Crypto Exchanges in Australia

Choosing a crypto exchange in Australia isn't just about finding the lowest headline trading fee.

The real differences show up in AUD deposits, PayID availability, trading fees, supported markets, regulation and access to products such as derivatives. A platform that works well for buying Bitcoin with Australian dollars may not be the best choice for active trading — and the cheapest-looking option can become more expensive depending on how you place the trade.

This section compares the crypto exchanges Australians commonly consider, with a focus on the things that actually affect local users: AUD funding, fees, regulation, liquidity and ease of use.

Which exchange should you choose?

There isn't one exchange that is automatically best for every Australian user.

If your priority is simple AUD deposits and a beginner-friendly interface, a local exchange may make more sense. If you care more about liquidity, trading pairs and lower-cost active trading, a larger global exchange may offer advantages.

Before choosing a platform, compare:

  • AUD deposit and withdrawal methods
  • Trading fees and spreads
  • Whether PayID or bank transfer is available
  • Liquidity on the markets you actually trade
  • AUSTRAC registration and any relevant AFSL coverage
  • Spot, margin and derivatives availability
  • Withdrawal fees and supported crypto networks
