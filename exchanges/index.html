Crypto Exchanges in Australia

Choosing a crypto exchange in Australia isn't just about finding the lowest headline trading fee.

The differences often show up in AUD deposits, PayID availability, trading fees, supported markets, regulation and access to products such as derivatives. A platform that works well for buying Bitcoin with Australian dollars may not suit active trading, while a low advertised fee doesn't always mean the lowest overall cost.

This section compares crypto exchanges commonly used or considered by Australians, with a focus on what matters locally: AUD funding, fees, regulation, liquidity and ease of use.

How to compare crypto exchanges

There isn't one exchange that fits every Australian user.

Someone who mainly wants to deposit AUD and buy Bitcoin occasionally may care most about PayID, bank transfers and a simple interface. A more active trader may care more about liquidity, available trading pairs, order types and trading fees.

Before choosing a platform, compare:

  • AUD deposit and withdrawal methods
  • Trading fees and spreads
  • PayID and bank-transfer availability
  • Liquidity on the markets you actually use
  • AUSTRAC registration and any relevant AFSL coverage
  • Spot, margin and derivatives availability
  • Crypto withdrawal fees and supported networks
