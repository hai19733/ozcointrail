Swyftx Review 2026: Fees, Spread & What Its AFSL Actually Covers

Last verified: 20 September 2026 · By OzCoinTrail Editorial Team

Swyftx is a Brisbane-based exchange that has become one of the most popular local platforms for Australians moving between AUD and crypto, and it won the Finder Award for Best Beginner Crypto Trading Platform in 2025. Its app is polished, PayID and bank deposits are free, and it lists a broad range of coins.

The trade-off is cost.

Swyftx charges a standard trading fee of 0.6% on both buys and sells, plus a separate, variable spread. For someone buying A$1,000 of Bitcoin, the fee plus spread means the total cost is meaningfully higher than the headline 0.6% on its own — and higher than the 0.10% you can get through CoinSpot Markets or Binance spot trading.

There's also a regulatory detail worth getting right. Swyftx Pty Ltd holds AFSL 568543, but Swyftx's own disclosures state it does not hold an AFSL for spot crypto — ordinary spot trading sits under its AUSTRAC registration instead, and its derivatives service runs through a separate company, Eightcap.

Fees explained: 0.6% plus a separate spread

Swyftx's cost structure is easy to misread, because there are two charges working at once.

  • Trading fee — 0.6%. This is the standard rate for both buys and sells on the retail tier (30-day trading volume under A$100,000). Higher-volume tiers reduce the fee, down to around 0.1% at the top tier.
  • Spread — separate and variable. On top of the 0.6% fee, Swyftx prices each trade with a spread between the buy and sell price. The spread is set by market liquidity and volatility, and your fee tier does not change it.

Why the spread matters more than the fee

The 0.6% fee is only half the picture. The spread is applied on top of the fee and varies constantly, so the all-in cost can be materially higher than the 0.6% headline fee — especially on smaller altcoins, where Swyftx's own live spread page shows gaps of several percentage points. Swyftx discloses the live spread before execution, so the most useful comparison is the final buy or sell price shown on the order screen before you confirm.

Put against the alternatives, Swyftx sits in the middle of the pack on cost. It's cheaper than CoinSpot's default 1% Instant Buy for casual purchases, but pricier than the 0.10% you can reach through CoinSpot Markets or Binance spot trading. Here's how the headline costs line up:

AUD deposits & withdrawals

Swyftx does not charge a fee for PayID or Australian bank-transfer deposits, and AUD withdrawals to an Australian bank account are also free. Crypto withdrawals are charged only the network (blockchain) fee. That's a meaningful edge over Binance's current position, where direct PayID deposits remain temporarily unavailable — if moving Australian dollars in and out without friction matters to you, Swyftx clears that bar comfortably.

Card funding is different. Swyftx's current fee schedule lists Stripe card deposits at 1.875%. If you use Banxa instead, Swyftx itself does not charge the deposit fee, but Banxa currently charges 1.99% plus a spread of up to 3.5%.

POLi is no longer available: Swyftx discontinued POLi deposits in September 2023. Swyftx added NZD bank deposits in January 2026, so it now serves New Zealand as well as Australia.

For the lowest-cost funding route, PayID or bank transfer is generally the cleaner starting point. The trading cost still depends on Swyftx's trading fee and the live spread shown before you confirm the order.

What you can trade

Swyftx remains primarily a spot-crypto platform, with hundreds of listed cryptocurrencies and tools such as recurring buys, Bundles, a demo mode with A$10,000 in virtual funds and TradingView charting. It's also one of the few Australian platforms that supports SMSF, company and trust accounts, which matters for people holding crypto in a self-managed super fund.

It also now provides access to crypto derivatives services for eligible clients. The derivative products are issued by Eightcap Pty Ltd (AFSL 391441) and distributed through Swyftx Pty Ltd (AFSL 568543), separate from Swyftx's ordinary spot-crypto exchange.

That distinction matters: the derivatives service should not be confused with Swyftx's ordinary spot exchange, and Swyftx itself states that it does not hold an AFSL for spot-crypto trading. Staking and Earn products were withdrawn in early 2023, so there's no yield feature today.

Regulation & safety

Swyftx is registered with AUSTRAC as a digital currency exchange, subject to Australia's AML/CTF framework including KYC and transaction monitoring. Swyftx Pty Ltd (ABN 72 623 556 730) also holds AFSL 568543 — but read the scope carefully, because this is where most write-ups go wrong.

The AFSL fine print

Swyftx Pty Ltd holds AFSL 568543, but that should not be read as an AFSL covering ordinary spot-crypto trading. Swyftx's own regulatory disclosures explicitly state that it does not hold an Australian Financial Services Licence for spot crypto. Its spot exchange instead operates under its AUSTRAC registration and Australia's AML/CTF framework.

For derivatives, the products are issued by Eightcap Pty Ltd (AFSL 391441) and distributed through Swyftx Pty Ltd (AFSL 568543). They are separate from Swyftx's ordinary spot-crypto exchange service.

So the important distinction is:

  • Spot crypto: AUSTRAC-registered exchange activity, not covered by an AFSL for spot trading.
  • Derivatives: products issued by Eightcap (AFSL 391441) and distributed through Swyftx (AFSL 568543).
  • AFSL 568543: should not be presented as general regulatory coverage for all activity on the Swyftx platform.

Swyftx is not an authorised deposit-taking institution, and digital assets held on the platform are not covered by the Financial Claims Scheme — the same position as other Australian crypto exchanges. On security, Swyftx states it holds the large majority of client assets in cold storage and cites ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certifications, Fireblocks custody and Chainalysis transaction monitoring. We did not identify a widely reported platform-level security breach in the sources reviewed for this article. None of that removes the usual exchange risk, so keep only what you're actively trading on-platform. Frequently asked questions

Is Swyftx safe and legal in Australia?

Swyftx is an AUSTRAC-registered digital currency exchange and has operated since 2018. We did not identify a widely reported platform-level security breach in the sources reviewed for this article. Swyftx states it holds the large majority of client assets in cold storage and cites ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certifications. Like any exchange, it's not a bank: there's no government compensation scheme, so only keep trading funds on-platform.

Does Swyftx have an AFSL?

Swyftx Pty Ltd holds AFSL 568543. That licence does not cover its ordinary spot-crypto exchange service. Derivative products available through the platform are issued by Eightcap Pty Ltd (AFSL 391441) and distributed by Swyftx.

What are Swyftx's fees?

Swyftx charges a standard 0.6% trading fee on buys and sells, plus a separate and variable spread. PayID and bank deposits are free, AUD withdrawals are free, and crypto withdrawals cost only the network fee. Card deposits are different: Stripe charges 1.875%, and Banxa carries a 1.99% fee plus a spread of up to 3.5%.

Is Swyftx better than CoinSpot?

It depends on how you trade. Swyftx's 0.6% fee plus spread is typically cheaper than CoinSpot's default 1% Instant Buy for casual purchases, and both offer free AUD deposits. But CoinSpot's 0.10% Markets order book is cheaper for active traders. Swyftx is often the more polished app; CoinSpot has the longer operating history. The two platforms are covered side by side in our CoinSpot review.

Can I deposit AUD into Swyftx for free?

Yes — Swyftx offers free AUD deposits via PayID and bank transfer, and free AUD withdrawals. Card deposits are different: Stripe card deposits are charged 1.875%, and the Banxa route carries a 1.99% fee plus a spread of up to 3.5%. POLi is no longer available.

Does Swyftx offer leverage or derivatives?

Yes. Swyftx now provides access to crypto derivatives services for eligible clients. The derivative products are issued by Eightcap Pty Ltd (AFSL 391441) and distributed through Swyftx Pty Ltd (AFSL 568543), separate from Swyftx's ordinary spot-crypto service. Eligibility, fees, leverage and product terms differ from spot trading, so review the relevant Product Disclosure Statement and client agreement before using the derivatives service.
