OKX Fees in Australia 2026: 0.70% Retail Spot, Withdrawals & VIP Tiers

Last verified: 29 September 2026 · By James Walker · Editor, OzCoinTrail

If you've seen websites saying OKX charges 0.08% for spot trading, that figure is real — but it is not the standard rate for most Australian retail users. For a regular OKX Australia retail account, the standard spot trading fee is 0.70% maker and 0.70% taker, apart from designated zero-fee pairs. Lower rates apply as you move through the VIP tiers, while OKX also maintains a separate fee schedule for eligible wholesale clients. This guide explains what Australians actually pay for spot trading, AUD deposits and withdrawals, crypto withdrawals, and how the retail and wholesale fee schedules differ. Why OKX Australia charges 0.70%, not 0.08%

The biggest source of confusion is that OKX does not use one universal Australian fee schedule. There are two categories:

  • Retail clients — the default classification for most everyday Australian users.
  • Wholesale clients — eligible professional or wholesale investors who meet separate qualification requirements.

For a standard retail account, OKX Australia currently lists 0.70% maker and 0.70% taker at the Regular tier. That rate applies to standard spot pairs unless the pair has specifically been designated as zero-fee. So if you create a normal personal OKX Australia account and make your first spot trade, 0.70% is the number you should use when estimating trading costs — not 0.08%.

All figures on this page come from OKX's Australian fee framework, checked on 29 September 2026. Fee schedules change, so confirm the current tier before a large trade.

AUD deposit fees

For bank-transfer deposits, OKX does not charge its own AUD deposit fee. Your bank or payment provider may still apply its own charge, so check the amount displayed before sending money. Card-funded transactions can have separate charges; OKX displays applicable costs before confirmation.

One important restriction is that AUD should not be sent directly to OKX from another cryptocurrency exchange. If you are moving Australian dollars from another exchange, the safer flow is generally crypto exchange → your Australian bank account → OKX, rather than sending fiat directly exchange-to-exchange. Our guide to depositing AUD into OKX covers the flow in detail.

AUD withdrawal fees

OKX states that it does not charge a fee when withdrawing AUD to an Australian bank account. That makes fiat withdrawals relatively straightforward compared with crypto withdrawals, where the blockchain network affects the cost. Always verify the amount shown in the withdrawal preview before confirming.

Crypto deposit fees

OKX does not charge a network fee for receiving a normal crypto deposit. Two other conditions still matter:

  • Minimum deposit — some assets or networks can have a minimum amount required for the deposit to be credited.
  • Correct network — the network you send from must match the network selected on the OKX deposit page. Do not assume two networks supporting the same token are interchangeable; copy the network and address directly from your OKX deposit screen.

Crypto withdrawal fees

Crypto withdrawals are different from AUD withdrawals — there is no single OKX crypto withdrawal fee. The amount varies based on the cryptocurrency, the selected blockchain network, current network conditions, and OKX's displayed withdrawal fee at the time. The app shows the fee before you confirm the withdrawal, so a static table of BTC, ETH or USDT withdrawal charges can become outdated quickly. Check the live withdrawal preview instead.

How to reduce your OKX trading costs

  1. Check whether the pair is zero-fee. OKX can designate selected spot pairs as zero-fee, in which case maker and taker fees may be 0.00%. The list can change, so check the current fee page rather than assuming a particular pair remains free.
  2. Understand maker vs taker orders. A maker order adds liquidity to the order book; a taker order immediately matches existing liquidity. At the Regular retail tier both cost 0.70%, but at higher VIP levels maker fees fall faster than taker fees.
  3. Don't judge Quick Buy only by the visible fee. Convenience features such as Quick Buy or Convert can include a spread in the quoted exchange rate, so a transaction can appear cheap while still producing a less favourable final execution price. Compare the amount of crypto you actually receive.
  4. Know your current VIP tier. Your tier can depend on either qualifying assets or 30-day trading volume. If you trade frequently or maintain a larger balance, check your actual tier rather than assuming the Regular rate still applies.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard OKX Australia spot fee?

For a regular Australian retail account, the standard spot fee is currently 0.70% maker and 0.70% taker, excluding designated zero-fee pairs.

Why do some websites say OKX charges 0.08%?

Different OKX fee schedules and VIP levels are often mixed together. The Australian retail schedule reaches a 0.08% maker fee only at VIP 5, while 0.08% / 0.10% also appears in the separate wholesale schedule. Neither is the starting rate for an ordinary retail account.

Does OKX charge for AUD deposits?

OKX does not charge its own fee for eligible AUD bank-transfer deposits. Your bank or payment provider may still charge a fee, and card-funded transactions can carry separate charges shown before you confirm.

Does OKX charge for AUD withdrawals?

OKX states that AUD withdrawals to an Australian bank account are free on its side.

What is the OKX crypto withdrawal fee?

There is no single fixed fee. It varies by cryptocurrency and blockchain network and is displayed on the withdrawal confirmation screen.

Can I get lower than the 0.70% retail fee?

Yes. Retail fees decrease at higher VIP tiers, and selected pairs can also be designated zero-fee. Qualification requirements apply.
