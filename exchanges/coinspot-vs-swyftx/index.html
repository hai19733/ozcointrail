CoinSpot vs Swyftx Australia 2026: Fees, Spread & Which Is Better

Last verified: 12 October 2026 · By James Walker · Editor, OzCoinTrail

CoinSpot vs Swyftx: which is cheaper and better for Australians in 2026? Swyftx starts at a 0.6% trading fee, while CoinSpot charges 1% for Instant Buy, Sell and Swap. However, CoinSpot Markets charges just 0.1% on supported trading pairs.

The important difference is how you buy crypto. Swyftx has a lower standard trading fee than CoinSpot Instant Buy, but both platforms' quoted prices can reflect a spread. CoinSpot Markets offers a lower published fee, although execution price, liquidity and available trading pairs still matter.

For Australian beginners, Swyftx is worth considering for its straightforward trading experience and demo account. CoinSpot is worth considering for its larger cryptocurrency selection and lower-fee Markets feature.

This comparison explains the fees, spreads, AUD deposits, security and regulatory differences — including what happens when you buy A$1,000 or A$10,000 worth of crypto.

CoinSpot vs Swyftx Fees: Which Is Cheaper?

CoinSpot has two substantially different retail trading fee structures. Instant Buy, Sell and Swap costs 1%, while trades made through CoinSpot Markets cost 0.1%. Swyftx starts at 0.6% for standard customers and reduces fees according to the customer's rolling 30-day trading volume.

Why the fee comparison does not tell the whole story

Trading fees and total trading costs are different. A platform may advertise a smaller percentage fee while presenting a less favourable execution price, and a market order can also experience slippage, particularly if available liquidity is limited.

For a meaningful comparison, use the same cryptocurrency, AUD amount and near-identical time on both platforms. Check three things before confirming:

  1. The amount of AUD being spent.
  2. The final quantity of cryptocurrency received.
  3. The explicit trading fee and quoted execution price.

Decision: if two platforms quote different BTC amounts for the same A$1,000 purchase, the quote delivering more BTC under equivalent conditions is the cheaper effective purchase — regardless of which has the smaller advertised trading fee.

CoinSpot vs Swyftx Spread: What Are You Really Paying?

A crypto spread is the difference between the buying price and the selling price, and it affects the effective price available when entering or exiting a position. Swyftx explicitly applies a variable spread in addition to its trading fee, which depends on the asset and prevailing market conditions, and it publishes indicative spreads on its website with figures updated regularly. CoinSpot's Instant Buy quote can also differ from its available market execution prices, and its 1% fee should not be interpreted as proof that buying and selling price differences have disappeared.

Trading fee vs spread

Cost component What it means
Trading feeExplicit charge for executing a trade
SpreadDifference between buying and selling prices
SlippageExecution difference caused by price movement or liquidity
Funding feePossible charge for the chosen deposit method

A trading fee is relatively straightforward to calculate, while spreads and slippage depend more on the asset, order type and market conditions.

Does Swyftx have a hidden spread?

Swyftx does charge a separate spread, but it also discloses its pricing approach and publishes indicative asset spreads, so calling this charge hidden would be misleading. The more useful question is whether its final quote is competitive. Some less-liquid cryptocurrencies have substantially wider indicative spreads than major assets, so a fee comparison based on Bitcoin should not automatically be applied to every altcoin.

How to compare real costs

Suppose you want to purchase A$1,000 of BTC. Open the buy confirmation screen on each platform and note the BTC amount offered, making sure the displayed amounts are net of any fees and that both quotes were obtained close together in time. The platform offering the greater BTC quantity for the same final AUD outlay has the better effective purchase quote in that comparison — this captures more than just the advertised trading fee.

Spread verdict: do not assume Swyftx is always cheaper because its ordinary trading fee is 0.6%, or that CoinSpot Markets always delivers the best execution because its fee is 0.1%. Compare the actual order prices.

However, not every cryptocurrency listed on CoinSpot necessarily has a corresponding AUD market available through the Markets feature.

CoinSpot is the stronger candidate when the variety of supported assets matters — but a larger coin list does not necessarily mean every token has a liquid AUD order book.

Can you trade crypto derivatives?

For ordinary spot cryptocurrency trading, neither platform should be treated as offering standard spot margin leverage. Swyftx provides access to certain derivative products for eligible customers through a separate issuer, Eightcap Pty Ltd, and eligibility, disclosures, fees and legal arrangements differ from normal spot purchases. Derivative trading involves additional risks, including leverage-related losses, and should not be confused with buying and owning spot Bitcoin.

CoinSpot suspended its previous Earn staking service in September 2023, while Swyftx previously withdrew its Earn product. These historical programmes should not be treated as currently available staking options without verifying a newly offered product and its eligibility terms.

CoinSpot vs Swyftx Safety: Which Is Safer?

Both platforms operate within Australia's digital currency exchange compliance framework, but neither should be treated as risk-free. Security needs to be evaluated separately from trading fees or the number of cryptocurrencies available. Relevant considerations include asset custody, withdrawal protections, account authentication, operational history and the risk of holding assets with a centralised provider.

AUSTRAC registration vs an AFSL

AUSTRAC registration and an Australian Financial Services Licence are not the same. AUSTRAC registration relates to anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing obligations, while an AFSL authorises specified financial services under Australian financial services legislation. Having an AFSL does not automatically mean a company's entire cryptocurrency spot trading business operates under that licence.

CoinSpot's AFSL explained

CoinSpot operates through Casey Block Services Pty Ltd, which holds AFSL 562554. Its published Financial Services Guide explains that the financial services provided under that licence relate to promoting and arranging the CoinSpot Mastercard, including specified general financial product advice associated with the card. That licence should not be presented as blanket ASIC financial-product authorisation for ordinary cryptocurrency spot trading.

Swyftx's AFSL explained

Swyftx Pty Ltd holds AFSL 568543, and Swyftx expressly states that its spot cryptocurrency exchange services are not provided under its AFSL. Certain crypto derivative products available through the Swyftx platform are issued by Eightcap Pty Ltd, which holds AFSL 391441. These distinctions matter when assessing what protections, disclosures and legal obligations apply to a particular service.

Security track records

Both exchanges describe controls intended to protect customer assets, including cold-storage arrangements and account security measures. CoinSpot has cited ISO 27001 certification, and public reporting in November 2023 identified an incident involving funds removed from a CoinSpot-linked hot wallet — the publicly reported incident should not be described as proof that every customer account was compromised. Swyftx also publishes information about security controls and certifications, including its custody arrangements and information security framework.

Neither platform should be considered equivalent to an Australian bank deposit account. Cryptocurrency assets and exchange balances are not generally covered by Australia's Financial Claims Scheme, and security practices and available protections can change, so users should review each platform's latest custody and account security disclosures.

Safety verdict: there is insufficient basis to declare either exchange categorically safer for every customer. Use strong account protection, independently verify withdrawal details and avoid keeping more assets on a centralised platform than needed for trading.

Can Australians and New Zealanders Use Both Platforms?

CoinSpot primarily serves Australian residents and is built around AUD funding. Swyftx serves Australian users and has expanded its New Zealand offering, including NZD funding arrangements. Eligibility depends on the customer's location, identity verification and the services available to that account, and New Zealand residents should check Swyftx's current NZ-specific conditions rather than assuming all Australian features apply identically.

Country verdict: CoinSpot is primarily an Australian platform, while Swyftx is relevant to customers in both Australia and New Zealand, subject to eligibility and local service availability.

Which Is Better for Beginners: CoinSpot or Swyftx?

Both exchanges offer interfaces designed to make buying cryptocurrency more accessible than a complex professional trading terminal. Swyftx offers demo trading, which can help users practise navigating orders before committing funds, while CoinSpot makes direct purchases straightforward — though users should understand the fee difference between Instant Buy and Markets.

For someone who wants a simple introduction without learning an order book, Swyftx may be the easier starting point. For someone willing to learn how supported Markets orders work, CoinSpot has a stronger published-fee proposition.

Beginner verdict: Swyftx is the more convenient starting recommendation for learning and straightforward buying, while CoinSpot is worth a closer look when lower-fee order-book trading or cryptocurrency selection is more important.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CoinSpot or Swyftx cheaper in 2026?

CoinSpot Markets charges a published 0.1% trading fee on supported markets. CoinSpot Instant Buy charges 1%, while Swyftx starts at 0.6% and reduces its rate through volume tiers. CoinSpot Markets has the lowest ordinary published starting fee of these options, but actual trading costs also depend on spreads, execution prices and liquidity.

Does Swyftx charge a spread on top of its 0.6% fee?

Yes. Swyftx applies a separate variable spread, and its published trading fee does not eliminate the effect of that spread. Review the final purchase quote before placing an order.

Why does CoinSpot charge both 0.1% and 1%?

Different CoinSpot trading features have different pricing structures. Its Markets feature charges 0.1% on supported trades, while Instant Buy, Sell and Swap charges 1%. Other order types can also have different fees.

Is CoinSpot Markets cheaper than Swyftx?

CoinSpot Markets has a lower published standard trading fee than Swyftx's 0.6% regular fee. However, this does not automatically guarantee a better execution price for every cryptocurrency and order size.

Which exchange is better for buying Bitcoin in Australia?

Swyftx is worth considering for straightforward purchases and demo trading. CoinSpot Markets is worth considering if you are comfortable using an order book and want the lower published fee. Compare actual BTC received for the same final AUD amount before choosing.

Is CoinSpot or Swyftx better for beginners?

Swyftx may be the easier place to start because of its demo environment and straightforward interface. CoinSpot is also beginner-friendly, although understanding the difference between Instant Buy and Markets can help users avoid unnecessary trading fees.

Does an AFSL cover crypto trading on CoinSpot and Swyftx?

Neither company's cited AFSL should be interpreted as blanket coverage for its ordinary spot cryptocurrency exchange services. CoinSpot's relevant financial services documentation concerns its Mastercard. Swyftx expressly states that its spot exchange services are not provided under its AFSL.

Can New Zealand residents use CoinSpot or Swyftx?

Swyftx offers services to eligible customers in Australia and New Zealand. CoinSpot primarily serves Australian residents. Check current geographic eligibility and funding methods before registering.

Can I deposit AUD for free?

Both exchanges publish free standard AUD bank transfer and PayID deposits. Card, cash and other payment methods may attract additional charges.

Is it safe to leave crypto on CoinSpot or Swyftx?

Both platforms publish information about their security practices, but centralised exchanges carry custody and operational risks. Users should enable strong authentication and consider whether funds not needed for trading should be held in a suitable self-custody arrangement.
