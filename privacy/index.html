Privacy Policy

OzCoinTrail respects your privacy and aims to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to operate this website.

You do not need to create an account or log in to read OzCoinTrail. We do not currently use Google Analytics, third-party advertising trackers or behavioural advertising cookies on this website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use OzCoinTrail and how we handle information you choose to provide to us.

Information you provide to us

OzCoinTrail does not require you to provide your name, address, financial information or other personal details simply to read the website.

If you contact us, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • the contents of your message; and
  • any other information you choose to include.

We use this information primarily to respond to your enquiry and deal with the matter you contacted us about.

We do not add people who contact us to a marketing mailing list unless they separately choose to subscribe to one if such a service is introduced in the future.

Technical information and website hosting

OzCoinTrail is currently hosted using GitHub Pages.

Like most website hosting services, GitHub may automatically process technical information when you visit the site.

GitHub states that the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites is logged and stored for security purposes.

Other technical information may also be processed by GitHub in accordance with its own privacy practices.

OzCoinTrail does not currently use this hosting information to build advertising profiles of individual visitors.

For information about GitHub’s handling of personal data, you can review the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies and analytics

OzCoinTrail does not currently use Google Analytics or third-party advertising trackers on this website.

We also do not currently place our own advertising cookies for behavioural targeting.

However, third-party services or websites that you visit through links from OzCoinTrail may use cookies, analytics technologies or other tracking tools.

Those technologies are controlled by the relevant third party and are subject to its own privacy policy.

If we introduce analytics, advertising technology or other tracking tools in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy where appropriate.

Affiliate links

Some links on OzCoinTrail are affiliate links.

When you click an affiliate link, you may be redirected to a third-party website such as a cryptocurrency exchange.

The destination website may receive technical information associated with your visit and may use cookies or other technologies to attribute referrals in accordance with its own privacy practices.

OzCoinTrail does not receive your exchange account password, identity verification documents or trading account credentials merely because you use one of our affiliate links.

For more information about our commercial relationships, read our Affiliate Disclosure.

External websites

OzCoinTrail links to third-party websites and services, including platforms such as Binance, OKX, CoinSpot and Swyftx.

When you leave OzCoinTrail and visit another website, that provider is responsible for its own collection and handling of personal information.

We do not control third-party websites and their:

  • privacy practices;
  • cookies;
  • analytics tools;
  • account registration processes; or
  • security practices.

You should review the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing personal information to it.

How we use personal information

Where you voluntarily provide personal information to OzCoinTrail, we may use it to:

  • respond to your enquiry;
  • investigate a correction or content issue you report;
  • respond to a privacy request or complaint;
  • maintain appropriate records relating to your enquiry; or
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not currently sell personal information or use contact enquiries for targeted advertising.

How long we keep information

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, including where retention is necessary for record-keeping, security or legal purposes.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

Australian privacy law

The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles apply to certain Australian organisations and businesses.

Some small businesses are exempt from parts of the Privacy Act, although exceptions may apply depending on the nature of the business and how personal information is handled.

OzCoinTrail aims to handle personal information responsibly and transparently and, where the Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles apply to us, to comply with the applicable requirements.

Access and correction

If you have provided personal information to OzCoinTrail and would like to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion where appropriate, please contact us.

We may need enough information to identify the relevant communication before we can respond to your request.

Privacy questions and complaints

If you have a question or concern about how OzCoinTrail has handled your personal information, please contact us.

We will review privacy enquiries and complaints and aim to respond within a reasonable period.

If Australian privacy law applies to the matter, you may also have rights to raise a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, hosting arrangements, tracking technologies or legal obligations change.

The current version will be published on this page together with the date it was last updated.

Contact

For questions about privacy or personal information, please contact us.
