How to Deposit AUD into OKX Australia (2026): Bank Transfer Fees & Limits

Public sources checked: 25 September 2026 (UTC) · By James Walker · Editor, OzCoinTrail

OKX supports AUD deposits from eligible Australian bank accounts by local bank transfer, and OKX states that it does not charge a fee for this deposit method. Your own bank may still charge a transfer fee.

There are several important restrictions: the transfer must come from a supported Australian bank account in your own name, and OKX does not support AUD deposits sent directly from cryptocurrency exchanges such as CoinSpot or from cross-border payment providers such as Wise.

This guide explains how to deposit AUD into OKX Australia, what fees and limits to check, how long a transfer can take, which payments may be rejected, and how the current AUD deposit route compares with Binance Australia.

Verification scope

This guide is based on OKX Australia's public help pages and fee information checked on 25 September 2026.

We have not completed an AUD bank-transfer deposit through an Australian OKX account ourselves. The payment methods, limits and instructions shown to you can vary by account eligibility and verification status, so always check the details displayed inside your own OKX account before sending money.

How to deposit AUD into OKX Australia

The basic process is straightforward, but it is worth checking the payment instructions each time rather than relying on bank details saved from an earlier transfer.

1. Complete identity verification

You need an eligible, identity-verified OKX account before using AUD cash-deposit features.

The payment methods available to you can depend on the identity and residency information associated with your account.

2. Select AUD as the deposit currency

On the OKX website, the route is generally:

Assets → Deposit → Australian Dollar (AUD)

In the app, go to the deposit section and select AUD under cash deposits.

Interface wording may change as OKX updates its app.

3. Choose bank transfer

Select the available AUD bank-transfer method.

OKX will display the banking details and any instructions that apply to your account.

4. Transfer from your own Australian bank account

Send the money from a supported Australian bank account held in the same name as your OKX account.

Do not send the payment directly from another cryptocurrency exchange or from an unsupported payment provider.

5. Check the instructions before confirming

Before sending the transfer, confirm:

  • recipient details
  • payment reference, if one is required
  • minimum deposit amount
  • deposit limit
  • fee information
  • expected processing time

Use the details currently shown by OKX rather than assuming details from an older deposit are still valid.

How much does an OKX AUD deposit cost?

For local AUD bank transfers, OKX states that it does not charge a deposit fee.

Your bank may still charge you depending on the account and transfer method you use.

The important distinction is that a free bank deposit does not mean buying cryptocurrency afterwards is free. Trading fees or spreads can still apply once the AUD reaches your OKX account.

How long does an OKX AUD deposit take?

OKX says most supported AUD bank deposits are processed quickly.

However, delays can occur with:

  • first-time deposits
  • large transfers
  • bank security checks
  • weekends
  • public holidays
  • incorrect transfer details

For a first deposit, OKX recommends allowing up to one business day.

Keep your bank transfer receipt until the AUD has appeared in your OKX account.

If the transfer has not arrived after the expected period, first check that you used a supported Australian bank and entered the current OKX payment details correctly.

What is the OKX AUD deposit limit?

There is no single public AUD deposit limit that should be assumed to apply to every OKX Australia account.

Instead, OKX displays the applicable limit inside the AUD deposit flow.

Your limit may depend on your account and verification status.

You can check it by opening the AUD deposit method and reviewing the payment details before sending money.

Can you increase the limit?

OKX also provides a Manage Limits option for eligible users.

A limit-increase request may require information such as:

  • occupation or industry
  • source of funds
  • expected deposit amount
  • reason for requesting a higher limit
  • supporting financial documents

OKX states that limit-increase applications are generally reviewed within one business day, although approval is not guaranteed.

This is more reliable than relying on a fixed deposit-limit figure quoted by a third-party website because your actual limit is the one displayed inside your account.

Which AUD transfers does OKX reject?

This is one of the most important parts of the deposit process.

Crypto exchange transfers

OKX states that AUD deposits sent directly from cryptocurrency exchange platforms such as CoinSpot are not supported.

For example, this route should not be used:

CoinSpot → OKX AUD bank details

If you hold AUD at CoinSpot and want to fund OKX using fiat, the usual route is:

CoinSpot → your Australian bank account → OKX

Alternatively, if appropriate for you, you can transfer supported cryptocurrency between exchanges instead of moving AUD.

See our CoinSpot to OKX transfer guide for the crypto-transfer route.

Cross-border payment providers

OKX also states that deposits from cross-border payment service providers such as Wise are not supported.

Use a supported local Australian bank account instead.

Third-party bank accounts

The name on the sending bank account should match the name on your OKX account.

Deposits sent from somebody else's bank account can be rejected.

Can you deposit AUD from CoinSpot to OKX?

Not directly as an AUD bank transfer.

OKX specifically lists cryptocurrency exchange platforms such as CoinSpot as unsupported sources for AUD cash deposits.

If you currently have AUD in CoinSpot, you can instead withdraw it to a bank account in your own name and then make a separate bank transfer to OKX.

The flow becomes:

CoinSpot → Australian bank account → OKX

If you already hold cryptocurrency rather than AUD, transferring supported crypto between the two exchanges may be another route.

Always verify the blockchain network and deposit address before making a crypto transfer.

After the deposit: what does it cost to trade?

Bank-transfer fees and trading fees are separate.

Once your AUD has arrived, the cost of buying cryptocurrency depends on the trading method and pair you use.

OKX Australia's current AU retail spot schedule lists the regular-user rate at:

Order typeAU retail regular rate
Maker0.70%
Taker0.70%

Different products or selected trading pairs can have different fee schedules.

For example, some stablecoin pairs may have separate pricing, while Buy/Sell or Convert can use a quoted price or spread instead of the same maker/taker structure used by the spot order book. The 0.08% / 0.10% rate often quoted for OKX is the global (non-Australian) framework, not the Australian retail schedule above.

Therefore, do not assume that every AUD-to-crypto transaction costs exactly 0.70%. Check the fee or quote shown for the exact transaction you are about to make.

Common reasons an AUD deposit does not arrive

If you have sent AUD but it has not appeared in OKX yet, check these points before sending another transfer.

It is your first deposit

A first-time deposit may take longer than later transfers. Allow up to one business day before assuming something has gone wrong.

The payment came from an unsupported sender

Transfers originating from another crypto exchange or an unsupported cross-border payment provider may be rejected.

The account names do not match

A transfer from a third-party bank account can fail because OKX requires the sending bank account name to match the OKX account.

Your bank delayed the transfer

Your bank may hold a first-time or larger payment for security checks. Weekends and public holidays can also affect processing.

You used old payment instructions

Always use the payment details displayed in your current OKX deposit screen. Do not assume that previously saved recipient details are still correct.
