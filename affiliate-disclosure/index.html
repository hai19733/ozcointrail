Affiliate Disclosure

OzCoinTrail is free to read. Some links on this website are affiliate links, which means we may receive a commission if you open an account, use a service or complete an eligible action after clicking one of those links.

Using an affiliate link generally does not create an additional charge from OzCoinTrail. However, fees, promotions, rebates, referral benefits and eligibility conditions are determined by the relevant platform and may change over time.

This page explains how these commercial relationships work and how we manage them in our editorial content.

How we earn money

OzCoinTrail may earn revenue through affiliate partnerships.

For example, if you click an affiliate link to Binance or OKX and subsequently open an account or complete a qualifying action, the platform may pay OzCoinTrail a commission.

The amount we receive may vary depending on the platform, product, campaign or qualifying activity.

You should always check the provider’s current terms, fees and eligibility requirements before opening an account, depositing funds or trading.

How affiliate relationships affect our content

Affiliate revenue helps support the operation of OzCoinTrail, but our aim is to separate commercial relationships from our research and editorial conclusions.

What we don’t do

  • We do not intentionally rank a platform more favourably because it pays a higher affiliate commission.
  • We do not intentionally remove or soften material disadvantages because a platform is an affiliate partner.
  • We do not guarantee that any exchange or service is suitable for every Australian user.
  • We do not present paid promotional content as independent editorial content.

If content is directly sponsored, paid for or produced as part of a commercial campaign, we will identify that relationship where appropriate.

What we do

  • We use official platform documentation, publicly available information and relevant Australian regulatory sources when researching articles.
  • We disclose affiliate relationships where they are relevant to the content.
  • We aim to place a clear disclosure near affiliate calls to action so readers can understand the commercial relationship before clicking.
  • We review important articles periodically because fees, products, eligibility requirements and Australian regulatory settings can change.
  • We may report disadvantages, restrictions or risks relating to an affiliate partner when they are relevant to the article.

Current affiliate partners

OzCoinTrail currently has affiliate relationships with:

  • Binance — referral and account sign-up links.
  • OKX — referral and account sign-up links.

We also publish information about platforms such as CoinSpot and Swyftx because they are relevant to Australian cryptocurrency users.

At the time this page was last reviewed, OzCoinTrail did not have affiliate relationships with CoinSpot or Swyftx.

Our commercial relationships may change. If they do, we aim to update this page and any relevant disclosures on affected articles.

How affiliate links are identified

Where practical, pages containing affiliate links include a disclosure close to the relevant link, button or call to action.

For example:

OzCoinTrail may earn a commission if you sign up through this link.

Affiliate links may also include HTML link attributes such as:

rel="sponsored"

or:

rel="nofollow sponsored"

These attributes help identify the commercial nature of a link to search engines but are not intended to replace a clear disclosure to readers.

Reviews and comparisons

An affiliate relationship does not guarantee positive coverage.

Depending on the article, our reviews and comparisons may consider factors such as:

  • trading fees
  • AUD deposit and withdrawal methods
  • PayID and bank-transfer availability
  • cryptocurrency withdrawal fees
  • account eligibility
  • Australian regulatory status
  • security features
  • customer support
  • product availability and limitations
  • risks and disadvantages identified during our research

Different users may place different weight on these factors, so a platform that suits one person may not suit another.

Where an important fact can change over time, we aim to state when the relevant information was last reviewed or verified.

Australian Consumer Law and commercial disclosures

OzCoinTrail aims to disclose commercial relationships clearly so readers are not given a misleading impression about why a platform, product or service is being mentioned.

The Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) provides guidance on online reviews and endorsements and notes that undisclosed commercial relationships can affect how consumers interpret reviews and promotional content.

Our disclosure practices are intended to make relevant affiliate and sponsorship relationships visible to readers.

If you believe a commercial relationship has not been disclosed clearly enough on an OzCoinTrail page, please contact us so we can review it.

Financial information and advice

OzCoinTrail provides educational and factual information about cryptocurrency exchanges, digital assets and related services.

Nothing on this website is intended to take into account your personal objectives, financial situation or needs.

Some cryptocurrency products and services involve substantial risk. Availability, regulation, tax treatment and consumer protections may also differ between products and providers and may change over time.

Before making a financial decision, consider your own circumstances and, where appropriate, seek advice from a suitably qualified professional.

For further information about the scope and limitations of our content, read our Disclaimer.
