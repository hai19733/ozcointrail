Affiliate Disclosure

OzCoinTrail is free to read. We may earn affiliate commissions when you open an account, use a service or complete certain qualifying actions through some of the links on this site.

This page explains how those relationships work and the safeguards we use to keep our editorial content independent.

How we earn money

Some links on OzCoinTrail are affiliate links.

If you click one of these links — for example, a link to Binance or OKX — and then open an account or complete an eligible action, the platform may pay OzCoinTrail a commission.

You generally do not pay OzCoinTrail anything for using these links. However, exchange fees, promotions, rebates and referral benefits can vary over time, so you should always check the platform’s current terms before signing up or trading.

How affiliate partnerships affect our content

Affiliate partnerships help fund the operation of OzCoinTrail, but they do not determine what we publish.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t rank an exchange more favourably simply because it pays us a higher commission.
  • We don’t remove or deliberately soften negative findings because a platform is an affiliate partner.
  • We don’t guarantee that an exchange is suitable for every Australian user.
  • We don’t publish paid promotional content disguised as independent editorial content.

If content is sponsored or paid for directly, we will clearly identify it as such.

What we do

  • We research reviews and comparisons using publicly available information, official exchange documentation and relevant Australian regulatory sources where appropriate.
  • We disclose commercial relationships where they are relevant to the content.
  • We aim to place affiliate disclosures close to calls to action and affiliate links so readers can understand the commercial relationship before clicking.
  • We periodically review articles because exchange fees, eligibility rules, products and Australian regulatory requirements can change.

Current affiliate partners

OzCoinTrail currently has affiliate relationships with:

  • Binance — referral and account sign-up links.
  • OKX — referral and account sign-up links.

We also cover platforms such as CoinSpot and Swyftx because they are relevant to Australian cryptocurrency users. We do not currently have affiliate relationships with CoinSpot or Swyftx.

Our commercial relationships may change over time. When they do, we will update this page and the relevant disclosures on affected articles.

How affiliate links are identified

Where practical, pages containing affiliate links include a short disclosure near the relevant link or call to action, such as:

We may earn a commission if you sign up through this link.

Affiliate links may also use attributes such as rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow sponsored" to identify their commercial nature to search engines.

Reviews and comparisons

An affiliate relationship does not guarantee positive coverage.

Our reviews may discuss issues including:

  • trading and deposit fees
  • AUD deposit and withdrawal methods
  • PayID and bank-transfer availability
  • cryptocurrency withdrawal fees
  • account eligibility
  • Australian regulatory status
  • customer support
  • security features
  • product limitations
  • risks or disadvantages we identify during our research

Where an important fact can change over time, we aim to show when the article was last reviewed or verified.

Australian Consumer Law and commercial disclosures

OzCoinTrail aims to present commercial relationships clearly and avoid creating a misleading impression about the independence of our content.

Australian Consumer Law prohibits false or misleading representations, and the ACCC has emphasised the importance of clearly disclosing commercial relationships that may be relevant to how consumers interpret reviews, endorsements and promotional content.

If you believe an affiliate relationship has not been disclosed clearly enough on any OzCoinTrail page, please contact us so we can review it.

Not financial advice

Nothing on OzCoinTrail is personal financial advice, investment advice, legal advice or tax advice.

Cryptocurrency and related products can involve substantial risk, and availability, regulation and consumer protections can differ between products and providers.

Before making a financial decision, consider your own circumstances and, where appropriate, seek advice from a suitably qualified professional.
