About

OzCoinTrail is an independent cryptocurrency guide for Australian readers, covering exchanges, AUD deposits, transfers, USDT, tax and regulation.

What OzCoinTrail is

OzCoinTrail is an information website. We research and explain the things an Australian needs to know before using a cryptocurrency exchange or moving money into and out of crypto — which platforms support AUD deposits, what fees actually cost, how PayID and bank transfers work, what the tax and regulatory rules say, and how to avoid the common mistakes.

Our content covers:

  • exchange reviews and comparisons;
  • AUD deposits and withdrawals;
  • crypto transfers and USDT; and
  • cryptocurrency tax and regulation in Australia.

Why we built it

Australian readers often have to rely on information that is not actually written for Australia. A platform’s global fee page may show one rate, while the AUD trading pairs available to Australians cost materially more. An AUSTRAC registration is sometimes described as if it were an Australian financial services licence. These mistakes matter, because they change a reader’s decision.

OzCoinTrail exists to check the Australian position against primary sources — platform documentation, ASIC, AUSTRAC and the ATO — and to explain it clearly.

Who we are

OzCoinTrail articles are written and maintained by named editors where appropriate. Each author page explains the topics they cover, how their research is conducted and the limits of their expertise. High-risk topics such as tax, regulation and SMSF are based primarily on official Australian sources and clearly distinguish educational information from professional advice.

Our editor is James Walker, who covers exchanges, AUD funding, transfers and Australian crypto regulation. You can read more about how he works on his author page.

How we work

We follow a written method for researching and checking what we publish. That method — what we evaluate, where we get our information and how we verify facts — is set out on our How We Review page.

Our editorial standards are set out on our Editorial Policy page.

How we’re funded

OzCoinTrail is funded through affiliate partnerships with some platforms. When you use certain links and go on to open an account or trade, we may earn a commission. These commercial relationships do not determine our conclusions.

We do not accept payment in exchange for positive coverage, and an affiliate relationship does not guarantee a platform positive coverage. Our affiliate relationships are disclosed in detail on our Affiliate Disclosure page.

What we don’t do

To be clear about our scope:

  • We are not a cryptocurrency exchange and we do not hold or manage funds.
  • We do not provide personal financial, legal or tax advice.
  • We will never ask you for your seed phrase, private keys or passwords.
  • We do not endorse any platform as safe or suitable for everyone.

For more on the scope and limitations of our content, read our Disclaimer.
